# Epochs to Eons
### A City Through Time

You've been promoted to Chief in Command of a prospering city. But for how long will you be able to see it through? 

Will you live out your years watching the city progress day by day? Or will you ensure you see it survive several millennia into the future?

You must guide a civilization through your own chosen time scale, balancing the growth of your city with the challenges of surviving increasingly severe disasters. As the Chief, you make the strategic decisions and manage resources needed for your city to stay standing throughout eons.

The theme of "scale" is reflected in how time progresses: you can fast-forward to skip the slow build-up, but larger time leaps bring bigger, more catastrophic events. Your choices determine whether your city thrives through time or crumbles under the weight of disasters.

Game created for GMTK Jam 2024.

All art and code was made in 5 days in the time of the game jam by Kalanoka, Cmoll24, and Strawfumi.
